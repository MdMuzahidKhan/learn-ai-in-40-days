# Day 24 Summary

## Topic
Embeddings and vector similarity.

## What I did
- I generated embeddings for 5 sentences.
- I computed cosine similarity between all sentence pairs.
- I observed which sentences the model thought were semantically close.

## What I learned
- An embedding is a vector of numbers that represents the meaning of text.
- Cosine similarity helps compare how close two sentence meanings are.
- Sentences about similar ideas had higher similarity than unrelated sentences.

## My observations
1. The cat and kitten sentences were close, which matched human intuition.
2. The two Python programming sentences were also close.
3. Animal sentences and programming sentences were much less similar.

## One-sentence explanation
An embedding is a vector that represents the meaning of a sentence so a machine can compare it with other sentences.
